<h2><br><img src="https://cdn.serilum.com/description/nameplate/about-the-mod.svg" alt="About the mod" width="838" height="64"></h2>
<p><span style="font-size:18px">Followers Teleport Too makes getting your wolves and cats out of sticky situations a lot easier. Currently in the vanilla game whenever you teleport, your followers will be left behind. With this mod you'll be able to easily bring your pets with you back to the base. Whenever you're teleported, via a command or by another mod like Waystones, it checks for non-sitting followers around you. If it finds any, they'll be teleported along to where you end up. The mount you're riding comes along too, and so do allays that like you.</span></p>

This can prevent fall damage or suffocation from an estimate target position.</span><br><br><span style="font-size:14px"><strong>durationInSecondsDamageShouldBeDisabled</strong></span><span style="font-size:12px;color:#8a8d91">&nbsp;&nbsp;default 20, min 0, max 86400</span><br><span style="font-size:13px">How long in seconds damage should be disabled for after a teleport when disableFollowerDamageAfterTeleport is enabled.</span><br><br><span style="font-size:14px"><strong>followerSearchRadiusInBlocks</strong></span><span style="font-size:12px;color:#8a8d91">&nbsp;&nbsp;default 50, min 1, max 256</span><br><span style="font-size:13px">How far around the player in blocks followers are taken along on a teleport.</span><br><br><span style="font-size:14px"><strong>followerTypesThatStayBehind</strong></span><span style="font-size:12px;color:#8a8d91">&nbsp;&nbsp;default "minecraft:nautilus"</span><br><span style="font-size:13px">A comma separated list of entity ids that never teleport with their owner, for example 'minecraft:nautilus,cobblemon:pokemon'.</span><br><br><span style="font-size:14px"><strong>followersFollowIntoOtherDimensions</strong></span><span style="font-size:12px;color:#8a8d91">&nbsp;&nbsp;default true</span><br><span style="font-size:13px">Whether followers also come along when a teleport takes the player into another dimension.</span><br><br><span style="font-size:14px"><strong>allaysTeleportToo</strong></span><span style="font-size:12px;color:#8a8d91">&nbsp;&nbsp;default true</span><br><span style="font-size:13px">Whether allays that like the player (after being given an item) teleport along as well.</span><br><br><span style="font-size:14px"><strong>mountTeleportsToo</strong></span><span style="font-size:12px;color:#8a8d91">&nbsp;&nbsp;default true</span><br><span style="font-size:13px">Whether the mount the player is riding teleports along, with the player put back on it.</span></p>
